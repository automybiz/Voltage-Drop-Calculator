The main purpose in mind when creating this voltage drop calculator was to achieve
the following 2 end goals while only making a single run with an extension cord combo:

    Goal #1: To most efficiently manage life support in an RV (Fridge/Freezer/AC).

    Goal #2: To add a business computer and 50 inch flat screen on top of life support.

The default Visible Midea Duo AC unit was chosen to start the calculator with because it hits the biggest bang for buck sweet spot in terms of power draw and cooling for my specific RV setup. It's a 10k BTU SACC unit that can cool up to 450 sq ft, which is more than enough for my RV. It also has an inverter compressor which allows it to adjust its power draw based on the cooling demand, making it more efficient and quieter compared to non-inverter units. The Midea Duo's ability to run in both Cool Mode and Fan Mode at different power levels provides flexibility in managing power consumption while still maintaining comfort. It also has a dual hose design which helps in efficient heat dissipation and eliminates a negative air pressure environment. Negative air pressure environments suck in hot air from outside which defeats the purpose of having an effective cooling system. Starting with this unit allows me to see how much watts/amps capacity I have left for additional products like the computer and flat screen, and helps me make informed decisions about cord combinations and potential upgrades in the future.
